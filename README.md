# HUL FY2026 Forecast vs Actual: Variance Analysis

A self-initiated FP&A case study using public data from Hindustan Unilever's annual reports.

## What this is
A forecast of HUL's FY2026 revenue and EBITDA built only from FY2022-FY2025 data, compared with the reported FY2026 actuals to calculate and explain the variances.

## Method
- Revenue: each of four segments (Home Care, Beauty & Personal Care, Foods, Others) grown at its FY2023-FY2025 average growth rate.
- Operating costs: FY2022-FY2025 average cost as a percentage of revenue.
- EBITDA = revenue - operating costs.
- Variance = actual - forecast, shown in rupees and percent, marked favourable or unfavourable.

## Data notes
- Figures are standalone, in Rs crore. All inputs reconcile to the totals printed in the reports.
- Beauty & Wellbeing and Personal Care are combined for all years, because they were one segment until FY2024.
- HUL's FY2026 report restates FY2025 after the demerger of the Ice Cream business, so the forecast starts from the restated FY2025 figure.

## Result
EBITDA was about 3.8% below forecast, mainly because revenue was below forecast, led by Home Care. HUL reports that price reductions in the earlier part of the year moderated Home Care sales growth.

## Limitations
Public data only, with no internal budget. The 3-year average growth includes an unusually strong FY2023.
